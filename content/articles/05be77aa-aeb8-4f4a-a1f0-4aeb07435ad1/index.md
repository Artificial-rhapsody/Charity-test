# Six Most Common Types of Telecom Fraud

Telecom fraud changes its appearance, but the pressure tactics behind it are often familiar.

## 1. Impersonation

Scammers may pretend to be police officers, teachers, bank staff, or delivery agents. **Verify through an official channel before acting.**

## 2. Fake refunds

A real merchant will not ask you to share a password or transfer money to complete a refund.

> Stop, verify, and ask a trusted adult before sending money.
